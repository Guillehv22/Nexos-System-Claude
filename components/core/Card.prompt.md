Rounded card (12pt radius) for comparisons, numbered process steps, or option lists. Matches tokens.json `components.card`.

```jsx
<Card fill="outline">Opción A</Card>
```

Fills: white (default, bordered), soft (light blue tint), blue (solid brand), dark (contrast surface), **outline** (no fill, blue border + blue text — for light backgrounds) and **outline-inverse** (no fill, white border + white text — for blue or dark backgrounds). The outline variants are transparent, so they take the colour of whatever background they sit on and stay harmonious with it. Never add a drop shadow or a colored left border.
